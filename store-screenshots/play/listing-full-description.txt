PDF Pocket helps you scan documents and work with PDF files on your Android phone.

SCAN DOCUMENTS
Capture pages, adjust corners, crop, rotate and apply filters. Save multiple pages as a PDF. Local Latin-text OCR can make scans searchable; check recognised text before relying on it.

ORGANISE PDF FILES
Merge files, split or extract pages, reorder and rotate pages, add page numbers and watermarks, and compress documents. Compression results depend on the source document.

FILL AND SIGN
Fill forms and add a drawn signature. A signature image is not a qualified digital-signature certificate.

LOCAL DOCUMENT PROCESSING
PDF processing runs on your device. Google Drive sync is optional. Sharing or exporting a file sends it only through the service or destination you choose. System backup behavior depends on your Android settings.

FREE TOOLS AND OPTIONAL PREMIUM
All included PDF tools are available in the free version. The free version may show advertisements. Optional Premium removes in-app ads. Where available, monthly and yearly plans show the total price and billing period before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play. Restore purchases and subscription-management controls are available in Settings.

OFFLINE TOOLS
Document tools can work offline. Advertising, purchases, subscription verification and optional Drive sync need connectivity. The built-in viewer is available on supported newer Android systems; older devices open PDFs in an external viewer.

KEEP YOUR ORIGINALS
Always keep backups and review processed documents before submitting or sharing them.
